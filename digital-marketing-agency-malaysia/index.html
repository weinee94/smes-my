What digital marketing agencies usually handle

Digital marketing agencies help SMEs get discovered, generate enquiries, and improve campaign performance. Scope can include search engine optimisation, paid ads, social media, content planning, landing pages, analytics, and monthly reporting.

How to compare agencies

  • Ask which channel they are strongest in, not only what they sell.
  • Review reporting samples and how leads are measured.
  • Confirm who owns ad accounts, tracking, website content, and creative assets.
  • Look for industry experience with local services, B2B, retail, F&B, or ecommerce.
