What accounting providers usually handle

SME accounting services typically cover transaction recording, bank reconciliation, monthly reports, debtor and creditor summaries, annual account preparation, and coordination with tax agents or auditors. The right provider should make your numbers easier to read, not only prepare files at year end.

When to outsource accounting

Growing paperwork

Outsource when receipts, invoices, payroll, SST, or inventory records are taking time away from sales and operations.

Need clearer reporting

Choose a provider that can give monthly management reports, not just annual accounts after the financial year closes.

How to compare firms

  • Ask what is included monthly and what is charged separately.
  • Check whether they support your accounting software or can recommend one.
  • Confirm response time, document process, and language support.
  • Look for experience with your industry, especially F&B, retail, ecommerce, construction, or professional services.
