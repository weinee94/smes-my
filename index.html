What to compare

Ask the right questions before hiring a provider.

Use these checks when you compare accounting firms, company secretaries, payroll teams, web designers, marketers, IT vendors, and other business service providers.

Check Why it matters What to ask
Company details Helps you know who you are dealing with before sharing documents or payment. Company name, website, registration details, and contact person
Response time Important if you have a filing deadline, campaign launch, payroll cycle, or urgent project. How soon can they reply and start work?
Languages Clear communication matters when discussing compliance, pricing, and business documents. English, BM, Mandarin, or Chinese dialect support
Service scope Prevents misunderstandings about what is included and what costs extra. Monthly scope, one-time fees, location coverage, and support limits
