Johor Bahru categories to build first

Packaging suppliers

JB has public supplier surfaces for carton boxes, paper pallets, food packaging, EPE foam, bubble wrap, die-cut boxes, and industrial packing. Good profiles should separate product category, stock vs custom, MOQ or quote method, delivery area, certification claim, and public contact route.

Electrical and M&E contractors

Electrical pages often show wiring, switchboard, DB, power points, CCTV, access systems, industrial M&E, or project portfolio signals. Good profiles should clarify residential vs commercial vs industrial fit, licence or registration claims where applicable, and site-visit requirements.

Company secretary and incorporation

JB company secretary firms usually bundle incorporation, statutory forms, annual returns, registered office, business address, and compliance reminders. Profiles should show whether the provider is a licensed company secretary or firm, what packages are included, and where responsibility remains with the client.

Payroll, accounting, and compliance

Payroll and accounting pages commonly mention EPF, SOCSO, PCB, Form E, EA, bookkeeping, SST, income tax, and business advisory. Profiles should compare monthly scope, statutory submission support, confidentiality, response channel, and whether the provider can support Singapore-linked or cross-border clients.

Buyer checks for JB providers

  • Ask whether the provider serves Johor Bahru city only, or also Skudai, Senai, Kempas, Masai, Gelang Patah, Kulai, Pasir Gudang, and wider Johor.
  • For packaging, compare MOQ, product material, sample process, print method, delivery timing, repeat-order support, and whether the supplier is better for SME orders or industrial procurement.
  • For electrical and M&E works, ask for a written scope, site visit process, safety responsibility, licence or registration evidence where applicable, warranty, and what is excluded from the quote.
  • For company secretary, payroll, and accounting, separate one-off incorporation work from monthly compliance, statutory submissions, registered office, payroll processing, and advisory support.
  • Check company details directly before appointment or payment. A listed source note is not the same as SMEs.MY verification.
