What payroll providers usually handle

Payroll service providers calculate salaries, deductions, overtime, allowances, commissions, statutory contributions, and payroll reports. Many also support payslip generation, employee records, leave summaries, and basic HR documentation.

When payroll outsourcing makes sense

Monthly compliance pressure

Outsourcing helps when EPF, SOCSO, EIS, PCB, and payroll deadlines are taking too much admin time.

Growing headcount

Teams with branches, shift staff, part-timers, or variable pay need a cleaner monthly process.

How to compare payroll vendors

  • Share headcount, pay cycle, allowance types, and overtime rules.
  • Ask whether statutory submissions are included or only prepared.
  • Confirm data security, approval workflow, and correction process.
  • Check whether HR advisory, contracts, or leave systems are available.

Popular locations

Payroll services are often available Malaysia-wide, with many providers serving SMEs in Kuala Lumpur, Selangor, Penang, and Johor Bahru remotely.
