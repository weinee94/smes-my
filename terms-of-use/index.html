Platform role

SMEs.MY helps Malaysian SMEs discover business service categories, submit quote requests, and connect with potentially relevant service providers. SMEs.MY is not automatically a party to any engagement between a buyer and a provider unless separately agreed in writing.

For SME buyers

You are responsible for checking provider credentials, scope, pricing, terms, and suitability before appointing any provider. You should avoid sharing sensitive documents until you are comfortable with the provider and engagement terms.

For service providers

Providers must submit accurate company, service, contact, and location information. SMEs.MY may review, edit, reject, remove, or pause listings and enquiry routing where information is incomplete, misleading, unsuitable, or no longer current.

Listings and enquiries

A listing or enquiry opportunity does not guarantee traffic, leads, sales, conversion, ranking, approval, or ongoing placement. SMEs.MY may change categories, matching rules, page layout, and listing criteria as the platform develops.

Acceptable use

Do not submit false, unlawful, abusive, spam, or misleading information. Do not use the platform to scrape data, impersonate others, or interfere with the site or lead capture systems.
