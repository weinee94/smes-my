# Talent × Market intake — Google-only backend

Public form: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScYofVzVrFY2hBHSfGb4TdXB-e_NMauZP1GdkBmVp8ak000cA/viewform

Form editor (private): https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1rlouCbKTgYZLTghaSENPsrgSCD0eX_8MZh4AmwhlJi0/edit

Response sheet (private): https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1UIKH5VV5eTK92Ew8jHJdpMHR1TaAgIS2vCkHnB1EJRU/edit#gid=38453449 — tab `Form Responses 1`.

The form is published to anyone with the link and does not require Google sign-in or automatically collect a Google account address. It requires a reply email and explicit consent. Google Forms appends answers to the linked Sheet and sends a new-response notification to `weineetan1994@gmail.com`. The notification is a reminder; the Sheet is the record of truth.

Columns A:G are controlled by Forms: timestamp, business type, enquiry source, current owner, bottleneck, reply email and consent. Do not reorder, rename or delete these columns. H:M are manual operations fields: status, next action, follow-up date, pilot interest, proposed price and money received. Status options: `new`, `contacted`, `qualified`, `pilot_proposed`, `won`, `lost`, `spam`, `test`. A blank status means unreviewed; check the Sheet even if no Gmail alert arrives.

Daily handling: review new rows, mark spam/test, then write one concrete next action and follow-up date for relevant enquiries. Contact only for the submitted issue. A paid pilot is `won` only after agreement; fill actual money received only after payment. Do not count the synthetic `TEST ONLY` row as demand or revenue. Keep old Accounting／Company Secretary leads separate and do not reuse their consent.
