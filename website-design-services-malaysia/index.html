What SME website design should cover

A useful SME website should make the business easy to understand, easy to contact, and easier to find in search. Design matters, but the page structure, service copy, mobile experience, speed, and enquiry flow matter more for local lead generation.

Common website projects

Company website

Best for service businesses that need credibility, clear service pages, contact forms, and Google search visibility.

Landing page or ecommerce

Useful for campaigns, product sales, bookings, promotions, and focused enquiries from ads or social media.

How to compare web designers

  • Review actual live websites, not only mockups.
  • Ask who writes the copy and handles SEO basics.
  • Confirm domain, hosting, email, maintenance, and ownership details.
  • Check whether the provider understands local Malaysian SME buyers.
