How SMEs.MY handles request and listing information.

SMEs.MY collects only the details needed to review quote requests, provider listing requests, and related enquiries.

Information we collect

For SME quote requests, we may collect service needed, location, business type, contact details, budget or urgency, and extra request details. For provider listing requests, we may collect company name, main service, location, website or profile links, languages, contact details, and service scope.

How we use information

We use submitted information to review requests, route suitable enquiries, contact providers, maintain provider records, improve service categories, and respond to support or administrative matters.

Sharing with providers

When you submit a quote request, SMEs.MY may share relevant request details with suitable suppliers, contractors, or service providers so they can respond. We do not promise that every request will be matched or that every provider will reply.

Storage and operations

Submissions may be stored in operational tools such as Google Sheets, email, or other systems used by SMEs.MY. Access should be limited to people involved in reviewing, matching, administration, or support.

Your choices

You may ask SMEs.MY to update, remove, or stop using submitted information where reasonably possible. Contact the administrator through the contact channel shown on the website or the email address used in your submission.
