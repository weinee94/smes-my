What SME packaging suppliers should clarify

Good packaging suppliers do more than quote a unit price. SMEs need to know minimum order quantity, material suitability, artwork requirements, sample costs, print method, lead time, delivery coverage, and whether the supplier can support repeat orders as sales volume changes.

Common packaging needs

Retail and ecommerce packaging

Carton boxes, courier mailers, stickers, product sleeves, thank-you cards, and custom printed packaging for online shops and physical retail.

F&B packaging

Takeaway containers, paper bags, cups, labels, sealing stickers, food-safe materials, and packaging that can handle delivery conditions.

How to compare packaging vendors

  • Ask for MOQ and price breaks at different quantities.
  • Confirm whether samples or previous production photos are available.
  • Check artwork file requirements before paying a deposit.
  • Compare lead time for first order and repeat orders separately.
  • Ask if delivery is included for your city or state.
