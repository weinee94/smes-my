How to use it

Good for fast sales replies before a quote.

Use this when a customer asks something messy like price, deadline, size, colour, delivery, or service scope. The goal is to reply faster and collect the details needed before you quote.

For customer chats

Paste the WhatsApp message or Facebook lead message directly into the box.

For voice notes

Paste the voice transcription, even if the wording is rough or incomplete.

For sales admin

Copy the brief into your CRM, quotation sheet, or team WhatsApp group.

For better follow-up

Use the suggested reply to ask only the missing questions before pricing.
