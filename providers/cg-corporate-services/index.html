Buyer checks before appointment

  • Ask who will act as the named company secretary and what licence or qualification applies.
  • Separate incorporation fee, secretary retainer, registered office, nominee director, payroll, and ad hoc compliance work.
  • Confirm statutory filing responsibilities, deadlines, document custody, and resignation or transfer terms.
  • Check the company details directly before appointment or payment.
