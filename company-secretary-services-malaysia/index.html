What company secretary services include

A company secretary keeps statutory records in order, prepares resolutions, handles selected SSM filings, maintains compliance calendars, and supports changes such as directors, shareholders, registered address, and company structure.

Common buying situations

New Sdn Bhd setup

Founders need incorporation guidance, name checks, initial statutory records, and clear next steps after registration.

Existing company cleanup

SMEs may need overdue filing support, clearer records, or a more responsive secretary after poor service.

How to choose a CoSec provider

  • Confirm what is included in the annual retainer.
  • Ask about turnaround time for resolutions and SSM submissions.
  • Check whether they can explain compliance in plain English, BM, or Mandarin.
  • Look for support with ownership changes, registered office, and directors' decisions.
