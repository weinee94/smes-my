Provider profile fields for SMEs.MY

A useful electrical contractor profile should separate what is known, what is invoice-backed, and what still needs direct buyer verification.

Identity

Display name, source document name, registration-style ID shown on source documents, service base, contact route, and source date.

Service scope

Visible work types, suitable property or project type, location coverage, and what information the buyer should provide before requesting a quote.

Trust signals

Source of information, whether details are independently verified by SMEs.MY, and any relevant registration or licence fields where applicable.

Public limits

Do not publish payment account details, private invoice line totals, private customer information, or claims that are not visible from the source material.

Buyer checks before appointment

  • Ask the provider to confirm current business registration, contact person, and service area.
  • Request a written quotation that separates labour, materials, hacking/coring, protection, and clearing work.
  • Confirm who is responsible for electrical safety, DB loading, patching, warranty, and post-work defects.
  • Do not treat this seed profile as an independently checked SMEs.MY listing.
